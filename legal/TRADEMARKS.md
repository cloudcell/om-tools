# OM Tools Brand and Trademark Guidance

This document adapts the approach of [`cloudcell/om-core/legal/TRADEMARKS.md`](https://github.com/cloudcell/om-core/blob/main/legal/TRADEMARKS.md) to OM Tools. **It does not claim that the name "OM Tools" is a registered trademark.**

## Names and ownership

`OM Tools` is the name of this extension and tooling project operated by Cloudcell Limited and its maintainers. `OM Core` is the name of the related engine; its mark and associated logos are governed by the [OM Core trademark policy](https://github.com/cloudcell/om-core/blob/main/legal/TRADEMARKS.md), which identifies Alexander Bikeyev as the trademark or potential trademark owner.

The software licenses for OM Tools and OM Core govern copyright permissions; they do **not** automatically grant permission to use project names, logos, or marks in ways that imply official status or endorsement.

No ownership or registered-trademark claim over the broader strings `Open Modeling`, `OpenModeling`, `OpenM`, or `OM` is made merely by this document.

## Generally acceptable uses

Truthful references to the project are welcome, including:

- Documentation or educational material describing OM Tools.
- Bug reports, reviews, comparisons, and community discussion.
- Accurate compatibility descriptions such as "works with OM Tools" or "integrates with OM Core".
- Clear descriptions of forks or derivatives that do not imply official sponsorship.

Such uses must not be misleading about origin, approval, affiliation, or commercial endorsement.

## Permission required

Obtain prior written permission from the relevant rights holder before using official project branding in a way that suggests an official product, certified partner, approved marketplace listing, proprietary "Pro/Enterprise" variant, managed service, or authorized distribution. Similarly, do not use confusingly similar logos, domains, handles, or product names that mislead users about their connection to OM Tools, OM Core, or Cloudcell Limited.

Published forks may comply with the software license while still needing distinct branding. Restrictions on branding are separate from rights granted by the AGPL.

## Attribution and contact

When practicable, describe OM Tools as a separate project that integrates with OM Core; do not imply that all third-party extensions are official.
