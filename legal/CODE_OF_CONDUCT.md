# OM Tools Code of Conduct

## Our standard

OM Tools is a technical project. Community participants should be respectful, direct, constructive, and focused on evidence and useful feedback about modeling software, extension code, runtime integration, documentation, and design.

## Expected behavior

- Criticize ideas, code, and designs rather than individuals.
- Give actionable feedback and state uncertainty openly.
- Assume good faith where reasonable and respect differences in experience.
- Keep issues and pull requests on topic, and respect maintainer decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discrimination, sexualized harassment, sustained disruptive trolling, impersonation, publication of another person's private information without permission, and abusive behavior toward contributors or maintainers are unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy covers OM Tools issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation work, project-operated communications, and project events. Conduct elsewhere may be relevant when it directly affects project safety and trust.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may warn participants, remove or edit abusive content, close discussions, restrict access, or reject contributions. Enforcement should be proportionate and based on relevant evidence.

## Reporting

Send conduct concerns and relevant links or context to `alex@cloudcell.nz` rather than posting sensitive details publicly. <!-- check-sensitive: allow -->
