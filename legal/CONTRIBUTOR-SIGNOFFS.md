## How to sign off on a contribution

1. Read the grant below carefully. If you need changes to its scope, request a formal CLA instead.
2. In a pull request you submit yourself, add your name, GitHub handle, date, and a link to that PR to the table above.
3. Include this exact affirmative statement in your PR description:

   `I agree to the Contributor Grant of License in legal/CONTRIBUTOR-SIGNOFFS.md for cloudcell/om-tools.`

4. If your work is subject to an employer or client agreement, include their authorization or arrange a signed CLA.
5. Sign off your commits using Git's `-s` flag, which creates a trailer such as:

   `Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>`

Maintainers must check that the self-submitted sign-off is genuine before merging. Never copy another person's signature, email, or purported acceptance into this file. A commit sign-off alone does not prove acceptance of the grant.

**Existing work:** If you want the grant to cover contributions made before your affirmative sign-off, say so expressly in your PR or use the formal CLA, which has a specific past-contributions clause. Do not assume a new project policy retroactively licenses earlier work.
