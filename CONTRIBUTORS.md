## How to be listed

Contributors may be credited here following review of work accepted into the project. To propose a correction or addition, open a pull request or contact the maintainers.

**Recognition is not a legal sign-off.** Appearance in this file does not establish that a contributor has signed a contributor license agreement, accepted a contributor grant, assigned copyright, or received repository write access. Legal acceptance is handled separately under [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [legal/CONTRIBUTOR-SIGNOFFS.md](legal/CONTRIBUTOR-SIGNOFFS.md).

GitHub's automatically generated contributor statistics are based on attributed commits; this manually maintained list supplements that record.
